About us

Engineering clarity in uncertain ground.

GeoScan Engineering is a multidisciplinary practice focused on subsurface investigation, field verification, and technical reporting. We serve clients working in construction, infrastructure, environmental management, transport, and resource development.

Our philosophy is simple: the best project decisions begin with accurate, site-specific data. We work closely with engineers, contractors, consultants, and public agencies to turn complex site conditions into practical solutions.
